# AuraPEQ FreeDSP Edition v0.1.1

A maintenance update for the Windows 11 x64 Portable FreeDSP edition.

- Added acknowledged Flash save progress and an in-page safety confirmation.
- Improved packaging and documentation.
- Removed private development material from distributed source.
- Preserved FreeDSP EQ and hardware-control behavior.

Flash saving may take about 10–20 seconds. Remove earphones and lower output volume before saving: a sudden loud sound has been reported after Flash completion. Its cause has not been established. Do not disconnect FreeDSP or close the launcher until completion or failure is reported.

Original Moondrop FreeDSP only: VID 35D8 / PID 1496. The bundled Windows native helper is required; this is not a browser-only edition. Readback is approximate. This update passed offline and loopback tests; no new physical Flash writes or isolated clean-PC compatibility test were performed.
